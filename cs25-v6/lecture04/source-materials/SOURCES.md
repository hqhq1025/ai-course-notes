## Source policy

- The official deck is the visual spine. All 106 pages were rendered and classified before prose writing.
- Seventy-five independent teaching states are required; 31 pages are intentional omissions because they are title/divider/thank-you pages, link-only pages, or progressive builds superseded by a complete state.
- The full 61:48 official recording was sampled every five seconds. The 742-frame audit found no independent deck-external teaching visual; classroom video is used only for teacher voice and Q&A.
- Claims tied to product/library behavior are framed as the lecture's 2026-04-23 snapshot and checked against the primary references above.

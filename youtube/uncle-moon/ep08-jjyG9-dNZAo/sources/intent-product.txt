Voice Cloning
Speak in Your Own Voice, in Any Language

Face to Face
Talk Face to Face, in Any Language
Set one phone between you and the person in front of you — the screen splits so you each read your own language. Speak naturally and every sentence is translated on the spot, so both of you understand each other instantly. Made for travelling, ordering, asking directions, or meeting someone new — no shared language required.

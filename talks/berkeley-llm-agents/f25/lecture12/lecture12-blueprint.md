## Teaching Arc

1. Agentic AI growth and the safety/security distinction.
2. Agentic hybrid systems, protected assets, and enlarged attack surfaces.
3. Attack chains from model outputs to SQL injection and remote code execution.
4. Direct and indirect prompt injection, memory/RAG poisoning, and system misuse.
5. Evaluation platforms and end-to-end black-box red teaming with AgentXploit.
6. Defense-in-depth, least privilege, safe-by-design, and secure-by-design.
7. Progent programmable privilege control and automatic privilege separation.
8. Monitoring, DataSentinel, information-flow controls, and provenance.

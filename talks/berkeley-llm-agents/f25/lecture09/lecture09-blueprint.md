## Teaching Arc

1. AI adoption speed and the shift from channels to a single conversational agent.
2. Outcome pricing and agents as products rather than technology demos.
3. The Agent Iceberg: workflows, data, observability, controls, and operations.
4. Relationship-building agents with memory and proactive engagement.
5. Build-test-optimize as the production development life cycle.
6. Voice architecture and domain-specific quality measurement.
7. Simulators, tau-bench, traces, insights, and expert answers.
8. Layered guardrails across enterprise systems, policies, supervisors, retrieval, and memory.

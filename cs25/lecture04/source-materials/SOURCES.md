## Source-boundary notes

- Stanford's V1 schedule lists this session on Monday, October 11, 2021, and the paper is a NeurIPS 2021 work. The recovered title slide visibly says “October 11, 2022,” while the Stanford Online upload metadata says 2022-07-13. Because these dates conflict, the note treats the course schedule and publication record as the historical anchor and records the visible slide date only as a source inconsistency.
- Experimental numbers are interpreted in the benchmark and dataset regimes shown in the lecture. They are not universal claims that sequence modeling dominates every offline-RL method.
- Return conditioning is a requested behavior signal, not a guarantee that the realized environmental return will equal the target.
- Occasional extrapolation beyond the best logged trajectory is presented as an observed phenomenon, not a dependable out-of-distribution guarantee.
- Video screenshots are evidence of the original lecture deck; no missing slide is reconstructed or fabricated.

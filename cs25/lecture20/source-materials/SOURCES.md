## Legacy Note Audit

The legacy note had no video URL, only one body figure, and no source manifest, teacher-voice ledger, or visual QA. It named MineDojo, MineCLIP, Voyager, Eureka, and VIMA, but collapsed their distinct roles into a short survey. The rewrite restores the lecture's causal chain: active embodied experience, the three ingredients for generalist agents, open-ended environment and internet knowledge, video-language reward learning, code-as-action with curriculum/memory/critique, reward-code evolution, and multimodal robot prompting.

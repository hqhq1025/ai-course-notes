## Source-use rules

- The 2021 classroom framing is preserved as historical teacher voice. Later model families are used only for conceptual extension and are dated explicitly.
- Video screenshots are evidence of the original lecture deck, not reconstructed or fabricated slides.
- The lecture is introductory; equations and implementation details added to make the note self-contained are marked as teaching expansion rather than claims that every detail appeared verbatim on screen.

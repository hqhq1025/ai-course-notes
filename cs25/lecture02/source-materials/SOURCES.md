## Source-use rules

- The lecture predates public ChatGPT and later Codex/Copilot generations; product claims remain a 2021 classroom snapshot.
- Generated-text and generated-image examples are used to study evaluation, not as evidence that likelihood alone guarantees truth, usefulness or safety.
- HumanEval evaluates functional correctness over a narrow benchmark; pass@k is not a universal software-engineering metric.

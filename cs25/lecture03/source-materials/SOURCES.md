## Source-use rules

- The lecture is a 2021-era account of visual representation learning. Later work may be used only as dated extension, not retroactively attributed to the speaker.
- VTAB, ImageNet, ObjectNet, few-shot transfer, and scaling plots are benchmark evidence, not proof of general-purpose visual intelligence.
- BiT and ViT results must be interpreted together with dataset size, deduplication, regularization, compute, and transfer protocol.
- Recovered video screenshots are evidence of the original lecture deck; no slide is reconstructed or fabricated.
